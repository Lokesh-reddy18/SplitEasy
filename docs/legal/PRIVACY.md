# SplitEasy Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 18 October 2026*

SplitEasy collects only what it needs to split expenses with your groups.

## What is stored
- **Your Google account basics:** your name, email address and a Google account ID, received when you sign in with Google through Firebase Authentication.
- **What you enter:** groups, member names, expenses, settlements and UPI IDs (yours, and guests' if you add them).

## Where it is stored
- **On your phone,** in the app's private storage.
- **In Google Cloud Firestore** (Firebase project in the asia-south1 region, Mumbai), so your groups sync across your devices and with other group members.

## Who can see it
- Your groups, expenses and settlements are visible only to members of the same group.
- Your UPI ID can be changed only by you and is visible only to people in groups you share.
- SplitEasy has no ads, doesn't sell data and doesn't use analytics or tracking.

## Payments
SplitEasy hands payment details to your UPI app. It never sees your bank details, UPI PIN or balances. It stores only the transaction reference your UPI app returns.

## Deleting your data
- **Sign out** removes your data from that phone.
- To delete your account and data from Firestore, open an issue at https://github.com/Lokesh-reddy18/SplitEasy/issues and it will be removed.

## Changes
This policy may change; the date above shows the latest version.
